## Site plan (unverified)
Crescent between Sin Ming Ave and Bright Hill Drive; central pool spine. Three clubs: Island Club (east, off Bright Hill Dr), Grand Clubhouse (central), Wellness Club (west, off Sin Ming Ave). 80+ facilities, several pools, tennis court. Luxury blocks 5 & 7 at the Sin Ming Ave end.

## Location
- Upper Thomson MRT (TE8): ~2-minute walk (confirmed). Bright Hill MRT (TE7, future CRL interchange) ~700 m.
- Ai Tong School within 1 km (confirmed).
- Thomson Plaza opposite (confirmed). MacRitchie Reservoir, Central Catchment, Windsor Nature Park, Lower/Upper Peirce (confirmed).
