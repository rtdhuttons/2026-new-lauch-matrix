# How to use the Thomson Reserve Stack & Unit Selector

The selector helps you find the right home at Thomson Reserve: which tower,
which stack and which floor, what it could cost, what you'll see from it, and
how it compares with other homes. It works on a phone, tablet or computer.

Open the link your Huttons Associate sent you. The page starts with a photo of
the development and its key facts. Tap **Explore stacks and floors** to start.

---

## 1. Find your home

At the top of "Find your home" is a filter bar.

- **By bedroom:** tap **All**, **2 bedroom**, **3 bedroom**, **4 bedroom** or
  **5 bedroom**.
- **By budget:** switch to "By budget" and tap a price limit, such as
  **Up to $3M**, or **Any budget**.
- **What matters most to you?** Tap any that apply: **Views**, **Quiet**,
  **Less afternoon sun**, **Near the MRT**, **Resale appeal**. These shape the
  recommendations further down the page.
- **Unit number:** type a unit such as **#12-25** and tap **Go** to jump
  straight to it.

The count on the right shows how many homes match. On the 3D model, homes that
don't match your filters fade out, so you can see at a glance where the homes
you want are.

## 2. The 3D model

- **Drag** to spin the development around. **Pinch** (or scroll) to zoom.
- **Tap any home** to select it. Its details appear in the dark panel beside
  the model (below it on a phone).
- Tap the **compass** or **Reset view** to go back to the starting view, with
  north up.
- **Colour by** changes what the colours mean: **Bedroom type**,
  **Collection** (Classic or Luxury), **Within budget**, **View clearance**
  or **Afternoon sun**. The key below the buttons explains the colours.
- **Surroundings** shows the real neighbourhood around the site from
  OpenStreetMap: roads with their names, nearby buildings, parks and the MRT
  station. Taller neighbours, such as the 21-storey blocks on Bright Hill
  Drive, are drawn at their recorded number of storeys. Turn it off to see
  the developer's full site plan on its own.
- **Flat plan** switches to a 2D site plan with overlays for views, noise,
  privacy and walking routes.
- **Facilities key** numbers the pools, clubs and other facilities.
- **Sun & shadows** shows where the sun is and the shadows it casts. Drag the
  time slider, press play to watch a whole day, or pick a month.

## 3. The selected home

The dark panel shows the home you tapped:

- the unit number, unit type and collection
- the **estimated price**, size in sq ft and price per sq ft (PSF)
- **− and +** buttons to step down or up a floor in the same stack
- which way the living room faces
- the view at this floor, and the floor where the view clears
- the walking time to Upper Thomson MRT
- the **floor plan**: tap it to see it full size

Tap **Add to compare** to put the home on your shortlist (up to three), or
**Full analysis** for everything about that stack.

## 4. Full analysis

This opens under the model and covers the selected stack in depth:

- **The stack, floor by floor:** every level with its price, coloured by view.
  The **View Clearance Floor Marker** shows the first floor where the main
  view clears what's in front of it. Use the floor slider or tap a floor.
- **See shadows at 4pm:** jumps to the 3D model with the afternoon shadows.
- **Set as reference:** makes this home the one others are compared against,
  so you can see how much more or less each floor costs.
- **Tabs:**
  - **Price & premium:** price, PSF and the extra paid per floor
  - **Sun & facing:** how much direct afternoon sun the living room and master
    bedroom get through the year
  - **View clearance:** what blocks the view, and from which floor it clears
  - **Noise & privacy:** nearby roads, pools and courts, and facing windows
  - **MRT access:** the walking route and time to the station
  - **Resale competition:** how many similar homes you'd compete with when
    selling

## 5. Prices

The developer's price list isn't out yet, so prices are **illustrative
estimates**, marked "est." everywhere.

- **Assumptions:** prices start at **$2,850 psf on the lowest floor** and rise
  **$15 psf for every floor**. You can change both. Or choose **Average PSF**
  and enter an average across all 1,268 homes, such as $2,900; the page works
  out the rest. **Reset** goes back to $2,850 + $15. Every price on the page
  updates as you type.
- **Table:** every level from 30 down to 1, with its PSF and the price of each
  unit type on that floor. **AVG** marks the level closest to the average PSF.
  A small corner triangle marks homes with a private enclosed space (PES). A
  blank cell means that type has no homes on that floor. On a phone, swipe
  sideways to see the larger homes.
- **Chart:** the price range of each unit type, from its lowest floor to its
  highest.
- The table and chart follow the bedroom filter at the top: pick 3 bedroom to
  see only 3-bedroom types.

Homes start at level 1 in the Luxury towers (Blocks 5 and 7) and level 2 in the
Classic towers (Blocks 1, 3, 9 and 11). Levels 22 to 30 are in the Luxury towers
only.

## 6. Life at Thomson Reserve and location

- **Gallery:** artist's impressions of the clubs, pools, gym, rooms and views.
  Tap a photo to see it larger, or **Show all 13 photos**.
- **Location:** the location map (tap to enlarge) and what's nearby: the MRT,
  the nature reserve, schools, shopping and food centres.

## 7. Recommendations

Three suggestions based on your filters and what matters to you:

- **Best fit for your lifestyle**
- **Lowest entry price**
- **Best supported value**

Each explains why it was picked and what you trade off. Open **Fine-tune your
preferences** to set your budget, how much extra you'd pay for a better
floor, and how much each factor matters.

## 8. Compare your shortlist

Up to three homes side by side: price, size, floor, view, sun, noise, MRT and
resale. On a phone, the bar at the bottom of the screen shows the selected home
and takes you to the comparison.

## 9. Profit by floor band

Does a higher floor pay off? This section shows what owners made when they
resold at **JadeScape**, a comparable development in District 20.

- **The table** groups 321 resales by floor: low (#01–#10), mid (#11–#20) and
  high (#21 and up), with the average and median profit, the gain per sq ft
  and the yearly return. The best band is marked **Top**.
- **The chart** shows every resale by floor. Bigger bubbles are bigger homes;
  hover over one for its details. The dashed line is the median profit and the
  dotted line the trend.
- **Why JadeScape is a fair guide** lists what the two developments share.

This record also feeds each home's **exit appeal** score: a Thomson Reserve
home on a floor band that did better at JadeScape scores higher. Past results
don't guarantee future ones.

## Good to know

- Prices are estimates until the developer releases the price list.
  Availability isn't known yet.
- View, sun and noise results are estimates from the site plan, the
  architect's brief, OpenStreetMap and TRM's on-site assessment. They are a
  guide, not a survey.
- Photos are artist's impressions.
- Always check the developer's brochure, price list and sale and purchase
  agreement.
